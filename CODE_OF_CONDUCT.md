# Code of Conduct

We want BMTC Live Tracker to be a welcoming, respectful place for everyone who
uses, contributes to, or discusses the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Keep feedback focused on the work and its impact, not on the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help resolve disagreements calmly.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not publish personal information without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
deliberate intimidation, and sharing another person's private information are
not acceptable. This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project
maintainer privately through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Adil-MohammedK). Please do not
raise sensitive reports in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports and may edit or remove content, close
discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled
as discreetly as practical.
